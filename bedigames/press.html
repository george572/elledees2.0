About Bedigames

Bedigames is a slot game provider redefining how players engage with slots. The studio rebuilds the slot from the inside out, starting with how a single spin feels and shaping every mechanic, sound and frame around it.

Its upcoming title, Catch’n Grab, lets players catch individual symbols as the reels spin, retry with a respin when they miss, and collect instant cash when a single cash symbol lands. Bedigames partners with aggregators, platform providers and operators in regulated markets.
