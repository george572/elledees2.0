Who we are

Bedigames (“we”, “us”) is a slot game provider and the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us about privacy at legal@bedigames.com.

What we collect

We keep data collection to a minimum. We collect:

  • Information you give us through the request access form or by email: your name, work email, company, company type, area of interest and anything you include in your message.
  • Technical information that your browser sends when you visit, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. This is kept in standard server logs for security and reliability.

How we use your data

  • To respond to your enquiry and evaluate a potential partnership.
  • To share demo builds, documentation and other materials you request.
  • To keep the Website secure and working properly.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

Legal basis

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we process your data on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries and running our Website, on the basis of your consent where you give it, and where necessary to comply with legal obligations.

Cookies and third parties

The Website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Our fonts are served by Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google servers and shares your IP address with Google when a page loads. We may also use trusted service providers for hosting and email, who process data only on our instructions.

International transfers

Some of our service providers may process data outside your country. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep data

We keep enquiry data for as long as needed to handle your request and any resulting business relationship, and then for up to 24 months unless a longer period is required by law. Server logs are kept for a short period for security purposes.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to or restrict how we use it;
  • receive a copy of it in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email legal@bedigames.com.

Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version.
