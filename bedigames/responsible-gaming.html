Responsible gaming

We build games to be enjoyed. Keeping play safe, fair and for adults only is part of how we design every one of them.

Our commitment

Adults only

Our games are intended only for players aged 18 or over, or the legal age in their jurisdiction if higher, in regulated markets.

Fair, certified play

Every game outcome is determined by certified random number generation, and our maths is tested to the standards of each market we enter.

Built-in safeguards

Our games are designed to work with operator tools such as reality checks, session limits, deposit and loss limits, and self-exclusion.

Play for fun, not for money

Healthy habits

  • Decide on a budget before you play, and stick to it.
  • Set a time limit and take regular breaks.
  • Treat any money spent as the cost of entertainment.
  • Never chase losses or play to solve financial problems.
  • Avoid playing when stressed, upset or under the influence.

Warning signs

  • Spending more time or money than you planned.
  • Borrowing money or selling things to keep playing.
  • Hiding your play from friends or family.
  • Feeling anxious or irritable when you are not playing.
  • Neglecting work, study or relationships.

Where to get help

If gambling has stopped being fun for you or someone you know, these independent organisations offer free and confidential support.

  • BeGambleAwareFree, confidential support and advice
  • GamCareSupport, information and counselling
  • Gamblers AnonymousPeer support meetings worldwide
  • National Council on Problem GamblingHelp and resources in the United States
